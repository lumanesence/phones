### How I got it
So, I had gotten this phone because my Samsung Galaxy ZFold 4's hinge had given out, causing the phone to shut off after being closed. After this started happening, I was forced onto a Motorla E20 before being given this for Christmas by my mum, and I'm still using it as my main to this day. It's a solid phone and all, and it's quite durable too with a nice battery life! Only gripe I do have about it is the microphone isn't that great (which results in watery audio) and the camera becomes extremely blurry as I zoom in.
